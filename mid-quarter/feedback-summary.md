# Mid-quarter class feedback and our response

This AI-assisted summary is based on the class/TA feedback described in the completed **Mid-Quarter Analysis (28 October 2026)**. It is not a verbatim feedback transcript or an independent record of every class comment.

## Feedback recorded in the analysis

Justify the metrics and explain how each demo tests the project hypothesis.

## What we changed

1. **Retired the unsupported 30% target.** We have no measured baseline of avoidable RFI submissions. The cited industry reports establish context, not the improvement our tool can achieve at Evergreen.
2. **Separated intermediate results from RFI outcomes.** Meeting-note preparation and issue detection do not directly measure whether an RFI should be submitted.
3. **Kept quality visible.** Demo 1 saved preparation time but had lower average accuracy than manual notes. A combined score must not hide that tradeoff.
4. **Qualified the Demo 2 results.** The human comparison covers ten reports and twelve categories. Thirteen generated questions are not thirteen unique issues. The reported 93.8% time reduction remains unverified because source documents disagree about what the 209 seconds covered, and verification time was excluded.
5. **Defined the next comparison.** Label questions before testing, use current and outdated authorized documents, and compare manual with AI-assisted review on unseen scenarios with balanced task order.
6. **Counted human work.** Include preparation, generation, checking, and correction in total time; report verification effort and model processing time separately.

## Revised hypothesis

AI-assisted retrieval of authorized documents, followed by human verification, will reduce avoidable RFI submissions and total review time without incorrectly closing questions requiring formal clarification.

The goals of 25% less total review time and 20% fewer missed issues remain provisional. Final accuracy must match or exceed manual review, with zero incorrect closures required in the test set. Necessary escalation, unsupported answers, duplicate findings, and earlier conflict discovery are separate measures.
