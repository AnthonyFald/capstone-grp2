THE HUMAN-STUDY HYPOTHESIS

Still to be tested with actual reviewers.

The target is at least 25% less total review time, 20% fewer missed issues, and no increase in false alarms versus human-only review. No human productivity benefit is established by the model trials.

Assisted time includes inference, verification and correction. First-use comparisons also include setup. Counterbalance task order, calibrate report difficulty, and collect independent annotations before making the comparison.
