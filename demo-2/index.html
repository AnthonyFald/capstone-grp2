What belongs in the file?

These are daily construction reports: work planned and completed, site conditions, deliveries, delays, inspections, questions, and follow-up actions. Export them from a spreadsheet as CSV, or use the sample JSON structure.

Use 1–30 entries, with one row or object per date, author, and trade or work area. Several entries can share a date. Keep related entries together so the review can follow changes over time.

Required columns: report_id (unique), report_date (YYYY-MM-DD), and at least one of work_completed or issue_or_update. The sample also includes optional author, area, schedule, actions, owners, quantities, and evidence fields. Blank optional fields are fine.

Upload CSV or JSON only, under 150 KB. PDF, Word, photos, and audio need to be converted to this structure first.
