# Uploading daily construction reports

Use daily site reports written by a supervisor, superintendent or trade foreman. They record what was planned and completed, inspections, problems, instructions, delays and remaining work. Each row is one report from one author or trade on one date. Include several dates and trades so later updates can be compared with earlier concerns.

Download [sample-daily-reports.csv](../data/sample-daily-reports.csv), open it in Excel or another spreadsheet, and upload the file unchanged to test the workflow. The [JSON download](../data/sample-daily-reports.json) contains the same ten synthetic Room B104 reports. Both follow the team's supplied **Simulated Daily Reports CSV - Daily Reports.csv**. They contain its 18 report fields and preserve blank cells; the supplied `report_completeness`, `ground_truth_issue_ids` and `ground_truth_notes` columns are excluded. The original annotated team CSV can also be uploaded: normalization drops those three annotation columns before inference. Uploads are treated as custom data, and the model does not receive research answers. See [source provenance](source-update-20261022.md) for exact input hashes and the retained earlier sample.

Required columns: `report_id` (unique), `report_date` (`YYYY-MM-DD`), and at least one of `work_completed` or `issue_or_update`. Other columns in the template provide useful context: `report_author`, `work_area`, `work_planned`, `schedule_status`, `rfi_change_order_refs`, `action_required`, `responsible_party`, `due_date`, `quantity_or_measurement` and `supporting_evidence`. Keep headings exactly as written. Leave unknown information blank; do not guess missing approvals or measurements.

Upload a UTF-8 CSV spreadsheet export or JSON using the sample's structure. This prototype does not read PDF, Word documents, photos or scanned forms. Use 1–30 reports per batch, a file under 150 KB and at most 42,000 normalized characters. Split larger projects into smaller coherent chronological batches. The page explains validation errors before analysis.

The model processes report text on your device. After analysis, each concern has a **Review question** and a suggested status that applies to that question only. “Resolved” inspection acceptance does not mean that a separate missing test result or formal decision record is resolved. Check the dated source timeline and choose Keep, Correct or Reject; add a note when needed. Consolidation attempts to combine repeated concerns while keeping their original evidence, but human review must still check its boundaries and completeness.

Download the **TXT review** to read or share the questions, scoped statuses, source quotations and your decisions/notes. Rejected findings stay labelled rather than disappearing from that record. The findings CSV is available for spreadsheet work. The full JSON preserves the complete analysis record, including inputs, original predictions and corrections, for later evaluation. TXT is an output format; uploads remain CSV or JSON only.
