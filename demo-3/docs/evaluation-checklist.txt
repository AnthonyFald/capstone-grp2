DEMO 3 — PROPOSED EVALUATION CHECKLIST
Future protocol; not a completed experiment or measured improvement.

[ ] 1. Freeze the experiment before collecting results.
Keep the RTX 5080 PC, Qwen 3.5 27B model/digest, quantization, runtime, sampling,
engine/prompts and timing boundaries fixed. Record case count, dates, rubric
and acceptance rules. Give both arms identical starting board snapshots and
inputs; neither inherits the other's changes. Preserve hashes and IDs.

[ ] 2. Obtain independent human labels and keep them hidden.
Have someone who did not tune the system label new permitted/synthetic cases
before seeing outputs; use a second person to resolve ambiguity. Label every
requirement, duplicate, distinct scope, escalation, answerability decision and
applicable approved/current source. Hide frozen labels from inference, lessons
and participants. Include changed deadlines, different locations, conflicting
records and obsolete approvals. Previously tuned cases are not untouched tests.

[ ] 3. Run a matched manual-versus-assisted comparison.
Balance participants and case order; avoid one person solving a case twice.
Both arms preserve requirements and make final simulated submit/do-not-submit
RFI decisions from equivalent records. Assisted users review every proposal
and record corrections. A separate scorer assesses anonymized outputs against
the frozen labels. Retain failures and unsuccessful attempts.

[ ] 4. Measure actual decisions, quality and complete workflow time.
Define one submission consistently. Count avoidable RFIs: questions already
answerable from authorized records available at that scenario time. Calculate:
  100 x (manual avoidable RFIs - assisted avoidable RFIs) / manual avoidable RFIs
Report raw counts and case denominators; zero manual avoidable RFIs makes the
percentage undefined. Thesis target: at least 30% reduction. Duplicate links
and shared groups are separate workflow counts, not avoided RFI submissions.
Score unsupported answers, incorrect closures, suppressed necessary RFIs,
merges, omissions, missed duplicates, retained qualifiers and exact citations.
Require zero incorrect closures/suppressed necessary RFIs and answer accuracy
at least equal to manual review; speed cannot offset these failures.
Time preparation, search, processing/waiting, checking and correction through
the final decision. Separate active human effort, end-to-end elapsed time and
model/load time. Website reviewSeconds/manualSeconds alone are incomplete.
Record assessed quality and raw manual/assisted unnecessary-RFI counts in the
review metrics; unassessed is not zero. Targets of 25% less total time and 20%
fewer missed issues, without more false alarms, are provisional team targets.

[ ] 5. Test retained lessons as a separate controlled comparison.
Propose a general lesson from reviewed development failures; a human approves
and publishes it. Compare the previous release (or none) with the candidate on
new hidden-label cases. Freeze code, prompts, model, inputs, board and rubric;
vary only lessons. Save release ID/version, approval and text hash. Exclude
answer keys and case-specific facts. Accept only a preselected improvement
without quality regressions; otherwise reject and retain the failed result.
Retire evaluation cases from untouched use once they inform tuning. Saved
corrections alone are not learning; model weights are not retrained.

[ ] 6. Demonstrate persistence through meaningful sessions over several days.
Proposed schedule: three actual days of new batches, revisions and corrections;
log dates, workload and outcomes. Exact instructor duration is unconfirmed.
Manually open the PC app and press On; press Off and close it afterward. No
automatic startup/unattended processing. Verify work queues while Off and
sources, tasks, decisions, lesson versions and history persist on reopening.
Uptime and repeated known examples do not demonstrate improvement.

Until these tests occur, make no independent-validation, learning-gain or
better-than-human claim. Synthetic evaluation is not field validation; the
thesis remains partly supported pending the direct RFI comparison.
Basis: November 2 source review and current consolidation workflow/metrics.
This is a proposed protocol, not an official rubric or completed result.
