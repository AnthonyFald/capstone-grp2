Runner versus publication labels

  frozen fixture bundle. Keep holdout as the fixture split for regression.
- publish-results.cjs --label and --phase apply to the immediately preceding
  --run. Use the informed phase explicitly for former-holdout reruns.
- The publisher now also detects informed review/manifest metadata and
  refuses an untouched/unseen evaluation label for those consumed cases.
- All selected runs need complete manifests, internally consistent scores,
  matching source/model provenance, and completed AI-agent reviews covering
  their exact selected case IDs and engine version. Missing or partial
  reviews and unfinished runs are rejected.
